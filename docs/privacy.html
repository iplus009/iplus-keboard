Keybird Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · App: Keybird (package com.iplus.keyboard)

1. What this covers

This policy covers the Keybird account and Theme Store features: "Continue with Google", email sign-in, buying or unlocking themes, fonts and sticker packs.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and an account ID, from Google Sign-In (basic profile scopes: openid, email, profile) or from email sign-up. If you sign up with email, your password is stored by our authentication provider (Supabase) in hashed form. We never see it in plain text after you submit it.
  • Purchases: the items you own, order IDs, payment method (bKash/Nagad), the transaction ID (TrxID) you submit, and the name and contact (phone/WhatsApp/Telegram) you give when ordering.
  • Device licence ID: a one-way hash derived from your device, used only to bind purchased items to your device.

3. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your purchased items saved to your account.
  • To verify payments and contact you about your order.
  • To prevent licence misuse.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use Google user data for anything other than signing you in to Keybird.

4. Where it is stored

Account and order data is stored with Supabase and Cloudflare, which act as our service providers. Sign-in tokens are also stored on your device.

5. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers above, or when required by law. Google user data is not transferred to anyone else. Keybird's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can sign out at any time inside the app. To delete your account and the data linked to it, message us on Telegram @boname3 and we will delete it. You can also revoke Keybird's access in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access).

7. Children

Keybird is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
